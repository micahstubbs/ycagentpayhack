Overview

This project demonstrates a complete invoice-backed lending marketplace where AI agents can obtain liquidity by leveraging invoice NFTs as collateral. The system bridges traditional finance (Stripe) with autonomous agent commerce (Locus + Base).

Why Locus?

Traditional payment systems (Stripe, PayPal) were designed for humans, not agents. Locus is purpose-built for autonomous agent commerce with fast USDC transfers, low fees on micro-payments, and programmatic APIs.
